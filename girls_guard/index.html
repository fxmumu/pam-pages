怎么守护你 How It Protects You

⏰

设置归家时间 Set a Return Time

约会前设置预计归家时间，App 开始倒计时。Set your expected return time and the countdown begins.

👤

紧急联系人 Emergency Contacts

添加多位联系人，每次约会任选其一作为守护者。Add multiple contacts, pick one per date.

✉

超时自动通知 Auto Alert on Timeout

超过预计时间未确认，自动发邮件通知紧急联系人。Email is sent automatically if you don't check in.

🔒

隐私优先 Privacy First

通过 Apple 登录，端到端加密。只有你和选中的联系人知情。Sign in with Apple. Only you and your chosen contact know.

📱

桌面小组件 Home Screen Widget

锁屏即可查看守护状态与剩余时间。Glance at your status right from the home screen.

⏳

延长时间 Extend Timer

想多待一会？一键 +15 分钟。Want to stay longer? Tap to add 15 minutes.
